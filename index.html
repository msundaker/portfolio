Global Content & Brand Leader

I've spent the better part of fifteen years educating global brands how to tell better stories, mostly by asking annoying questions until the real one shows up. I started as a journalist, which explains why I still can't leave a good "why" alone, and ended up running content, creative and strategy for an outdoor brand people tattoo on themselves. I co-own a business school, because apparently one job wasn't chaotic enough. Along the way I've shaped campaigns and thinking for adidas, IKEA, Dove and Tommy Hilfiger, and led enough workshops to have very strong opinions about whiteboard markers. What I'm best at: turning a room full of opinions into one clear direction, and getting people genuinely excited to run at it. Based in Zurich. Allergic to weak briefs.
